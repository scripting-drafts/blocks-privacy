Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Blocks is an offline puzzle game for Android published by toomanypatterns (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the game does and does not do with information.

No collection, no tracking

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The game contains no accounts, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or third-party SDKs.

Local game data

The game stores two preferences in its private app storage on your device:

  • your best score;
  • whether sound and haptic feedback are enabled.

This information never leaves the device through the app, and the app has no internet permission. If you have Android Backup enabled on your device, the operating system may include app preferences in your device backup, under your own Google account settings. You can remove the data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

Permissions

The only permission the game requests is android.permission.VIBRATE, used for optional haptic feedback. It can be turned off with the in-game sound/haptics toggle.

Children

Blocks is not directed to children under 13. Because the game collects no data, there is no personal information from children for us to collect or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will be reflected in a new version of the game or its store listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email creative.gsp@gmail.com.

Blocks works completely offline. There are no ads, no purchases, and no tracking.
